# Archipelago-WorldofWarcraft-Leveling

An [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) randomizer for **World of Warcraft**, played as a
leveling race through Vanilla, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King, Cataclysm, or
Mists of Pandaria (your choice) content.
Reach the level cap for your chosen expansion, or race to dig up a stash of Gold instead --
solo or in a multiworld.

* See [how randomization works](worlds/wow_leveling/docs/en_WorldofWarcraftLeveling.md)
  (goal, what gets shuffled, how zones and level caps unlock)
* Follow the [setup guide](worlds/wow_leveling/docs/setup_en.md) (apworld install, WoW
  addon install, connecting to a room)
